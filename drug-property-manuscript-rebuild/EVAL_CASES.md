## Suggested local acceptance experiment

Choose one Introduction span, one Methods span, one Results paragraph, one architecture figure, and one table from the real project. Freeze the input fact sheet and budget. Generate baseline and v2 outputs separately. Record reader preference with version labels hidden where feasible, concrete defects, and factual errors. Do not infer broad reliability from one manuscript or from a subset of successful units.
