# Prose transformation: select and order information before polishing it

## Establish the content that cannot change

Create a compact fact sheet for the current span: exact task, actual method, verified result source, authorized claim scope, related-work evidence, and unresolved points. Keep draft-reported but unchecked facts labeled. No new scientific premise may be invented to create a dramatic opening.

Identify what the reader should know after this span that they did not know before. A paragraph that only announces an experiment, lists modules, or repeats that the method is effective usually needs a more specific purpose—not fancier wording.

## Draft from a functional outline

Write the central proposition in plain language. Select evidence needed to support it. Decide what belongs in a figure/table, what must remain in prose, and what may move to a supplement. Only then write connected prose in independent language.

Use selected reference cards, not source sentences. Avoid word-for-word syntactic substitution. A general rhetorical move is transferable; another paper's distinctive expression, problem diagnosis, findings, and contribution are not automatically transferable.

### Introduction

Locate the actual methodological difficulty early enough for the intended audience. Define the nearest relevant capability before criticizing its limit. Explain why that limit matters for this study's task and how the design addresses it. Finish with the scope of the completed evidence.

Do not fabricate a general failure of existing methods. Distinguish an empirically observed limitation, a literature-supported limitation, and a motivating possibility. A reasonable empirical comparison can motivate a paper without claiming a universal theoretical deficiency.

### Methods

Explain the design principle before drowning the reader in implementation. State input, output, operation, and rationale at the right abstraction level, then equations and essential detail. Map equations and symbols to the actual implementation. Give standard building blocks only the detail needed to understand or reproduce the contribution.

A module list becomes an explanation when the text tells the reader what information is transformed or exchanged, why that transformation is needed, and what the next stage can do with it. A simple actual method should remain simple; do not inflate it with jargon or decorative mathematics.

### Results

Choose the specific empirical question and answer it directly. Provide enough setting for the comparison to be interpretable. Select a small number of representative values when needed; leave the complete matrix in the table. Explain the pattern and relevant exceptions. Distinguish numerical superiority, uncertainty, robustness, and causal attribution.

Do not make every subsection end with the same praise or caveat. Place important qualifications where they constrain inference. Interpret a finding only as far as the evidence allows. If the available evidence establishes only a performance difference, that is a valid bounded observation.

### Discussion and abstract

Separate what was observed, a plausible explanation, practical scope, and unresolved questions. Derive the abstract from the stable paper. Do not add benefits or validation absent from Results. A title names the actual technical contribution, not an aspirational brand.

## A separate sentence-level pass

Check the subject of each sentence, referents, given-to-new information flow, unnecessary nominalization, and redundant context. Prefer an explicit technical noun over “this” when the referent is ambiguous. Put the important contrast where readers can find it. Vary sentence length according to complexity; no fixed sentence-length target is mandatory.

Remove stacked abstractions such as “comprehensive utilization of synergistic multidimensional information” when the actual operation can be named. Remove vague superiority claims. Preserve valid technical terms instead of cycling through synonyms. Not every sentence needs “however,” “moreover,” or “therefore”; the relation must be logically real.

The rewritten paragraph should sound like an author explaining an actual result, not an evaluator listing its virtues. Do not use AI-detector scores as a writing target.

## Validate the transformation

Reverse-outline the new paragraph, not just the plan. Does each sentence advance its purpose? Is the transition to the next paragraph real? Can a reader locate the observation and separate it from interpretation? Did a stronger verb accidentally strengthen the scientific claim?

Show a few useful before/after pairs in the editorial report with concrete reasons. For a local unit, offer two candidates only when they embody meaningfully different information organizations. Do not generate many near-identical synonyms and label them alternative strategies.
