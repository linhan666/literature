# Codex launch prompts

## From completed narrative-critical review

`$review-presentation-designer` Please read manuscript_final.md (or .docx), references.bib, evidence.csv, claims.csv and all figures. Build a 15–20-minute review seminar presentation. Keep source-reported findings distinct from author-proposed taxonomy and inference. Prioritize a cohesive argument, method comparison, matched/conditional comparisons, contradictory evidence and research gaps. Build and visually audit editable PowerPoint. Do not invent sources, counts, lineage, statistics or experimental confirmation.

## From a review without evidence matrix

`$review-presentation-designer` First extract a draft sources ledger and claims ledger from the manuscript; mark unverified where originals are unavailable. Do not imply source verification. Produce an editable and elegant deck only from traceable or explicitly qualified claims. Include a visible unresolved evidence slide.

## Technical survey

`$review-presentation-designer` Make a taxonomy and mechanism-oriented technical-review deck. Visualize input/output, information transfer, supervision and test objects; compare against a five-axis comparability contract. Keep system-level observations distinct from component-level causal attribution.

## Systematic/scoping review

`$review-presentation-designer` Read the documented protocol/search logs/screening tables. Include flow counts only when recorded and source-verified. Respect the actual review type. Use evidence-distribution plots and risk-of-bias only where the records support them.

## Revise existing slides

`$review-presentation-designer` Review existing.pptx and the underlying manuscript for scientific traceability and visual quality. Preserve correct scientific claims and figures; reconstruct dense slides as editable diagrams. Run source audit, render, and page-by-page QA. Deliver both a redline/change log and the polished PPTX.
